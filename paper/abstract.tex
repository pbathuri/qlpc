\begin{abstract}
We present a fully state-resolved, IBM-Qiskit implementation of the quantum core of QLPC, a personalized
portfolio constructor, and use it to make a NISQ optimizer's working process legible and to bridge
natural language with quantum optimization in finance.
\keywords{QAOA \and Quantum optimization \and LLM-conditioning \and Quantum finance.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and Approach}
Variational quantum optimizers (QAOA) are reported as black boxes---one bitstring, one approximation
ratio---hiding \emph{how} the quantum state decides, and offering no port for the language-shaped views
that drive real allocation. We re-implement QLPC's quantum core, instrumented so every intermediate state
is observable. (1)~\textbf{State-evolution visualization.} Cardinality-constrained Markowitz selection is
mapped to a diagonal Ising Hamiltonian and solved with QAOA~\cite{farhi2014qaoa}; we snapshot the full
statevector after the initial superposition and after every cost/mixer layer, rendering the probability
over all $2^n$ candidate portfolios as it concentrates onto the optimum. (2)~\textbf{Language-conditioned
QAOA (the QNLP bridge).} An LLM emits a signed per-asset conviction from natural-language views; we tilt
the expected-return vector by it, deforming the cost Hamiltonian, so that rising linguistic conviction
continuously migrates the quantum ground state across portfolios. (3)~\textbf{Quantum risk.} An Iterative
Quantum Amplitude Estimation circuit~\cite{woerner2019risk,grant2021iqae} loads a fat-tailed loss
distribution and estimates the tail/CVaR.

\section{Results}
All experiments are exact on the statevector simulator with fixed seeds (well-separated 4-asset instance,
hold 2). \emph{State evolution:} QAOA at depth $p{=}2$ drives P(optimum) from $1/16=0.062$ to $0.63$
(layers $0.062\!\to\!0.25\!\to\!0.63$)---a $10\times$ concentration over a uniform guess---with
approximation ratio $0.92$ and best-sample recovery of the exact optimum; $p{=}4$ reaches P(opt)$=0.75$.
\emph{Language steering:} a graded LLM bullish view on the two lower-return assets migrates the optimum
$\{$A,B$\}\!\to\!\{$A,C$\}\!\to\!\{$C,D$\}$, and the QAOA modal selection tracks the language-shifted
optimum in all three regimes---the selection is a continuous function of language (Fig.~\ref{fig:lang}).
\emph{Risk:} IQAE recovers the tail-exceedance probability to within $0.07\%$ of the loaded distribution.
To our knowledge this is the first demonstration of a natural-language view \emph{visibly} deforming the
state evolution of a quantum portfolio optimizer.

\paragraph{Scope (stated up front).} Small, well-separated, exactly-simulated instances; no scaling or
supremacy claim (brute force is the oracle); additive language tilt; asymptotic (not wall-clock) IQAE
advantage. The contribution is transparency and a language$\leftrightarrow$quantum$\leftrightarrow$finance
bridge. Code and figures are reproducible from \texttt{python -m paper.experiments} (Qiskit~2.x + Aer).
