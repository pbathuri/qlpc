\begin{abstract}
Variational quantum optimizers such as QAOA are usually judged by a single scalar---the approximation
ratio---which hides \emph{how} the quantum state reaches its answer and leaves no room for the
qualitative, language-shaped views that drive real financial decisions. We present a fully
state-resolved, IBM-Qiskit implementation of the quantum core of QLPC, a personalized portfolio
constructor. First, we expose the \emph{evolution of the quantum state} through every QAOA layer of a
cardinality-constrained Markowitz problem, showing probability mass migrate from a uniform superposition
onto the optimal portfolio (P(optimum) $=\Puniform\to\Popt$ at depth $p{=}2$, a $\Conc\times$
concentration). Second, we introduce \emph{language-conditioned QAOA}: an LLM-extracted natural-language
view is encoded as a tilt on expected returns, deforming the cost Hamiltonian and visibly
re-concentrating the quantum ground state on a different portfolio as conviction rises. We pair the
optimizer with an Iterative Quantum Amplitude Estimation circuit reproducing the Woerner--Egger tail/CVaR
primitive. All experiments are exact on the statevector simulator with fixed seeds, giving a transparent,
auditable bridge between language, quantum optimization, and finance.
\keywords{QAOA \and Quantum optimization \and Portfolio selection \and Amplitude estimation \and
LLM-conditioning \and Quantum finance.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Quantum finance has focused on two kernels classical convex solvers handle poorly: discrete,
cardinality-constrained \emph{selection}, attacked with QAOA~\cite{farhi2014qaoa}, and \emph{tail-risk
estimation}, where amplitude estimation gives a quadratic sampling advantage over Monte-Carlo
\cite{brassard2002amplitude,woerner2019risk,egger2020finance}. Two gaps persist. (i)~These optimizers are
reported as black boxes---a bitstring and a ratio---with no view of the \emph{state evolution} that
produced them, unhelpful where ``no room for error'' demands an auditable process. (ii)~Real allocation
is driven by \emph{language}---theses, filings, news---which has no natural port into a QUBO.

We close both gaps with an instrumented re-implementation of the quantum core of QLPC (Quantum--LLM
Personalized portfolio Constructor). Our contributions are: (1)~\textbf{state-evolution visualization} of
QAOA portfolio selection, snapshotting the full statevector after every layer and quantifying
concentration on the optimum; (2)~\textbf{language-conditioned QAOA}, where an LLM conviction vector
reshapes the Ising cost Hamiltonian and the quantum ground state migrates continuously with linguistic
conviction; and (3)~a \textbf{reproducible IQAE risk replication}. All results are exact on small,
well-separated instances with fixed seeds.

\section{Method}
\paragraph{Selection as an Ising Hamiltonian.}
Holding exactly $B$ of $n$ assets to maximize $f(x)=\mu^\top x - q\,x^\top\Sigma x$, $x\in\{0,1\}^n$
\cite{markowitz1952}, we minimize $C(x) = -\mu^\top x + q\,x^\top \Sigma x + P(\sum_i x_i - B)^2$ and
substitute $x_i=(1-z_i)/2$ to obtain a diagonal Ising Hamiltonian $H_C$ over $I,Z_i,Z_iZ_j$ terms. The
penalty $P$ is set \emph{just} above the largest marginal objective gain of adding or removing one asset:
over-penalizing creates a near-degenerate feasible band that shallow QAOA cannot resolve, hiding the
objective gradient from the optimizer.

\paragraph{QAOA and state snapshots.}
We prepare $|+\rangle^{n}$ and apply, per layer, $e^{-i\gamma_k H_C}$ then the transverse-field mixer
$e^{-i\beta_k\sum_j X_j}$. As $H_C$ is diagonal, the Trotterized \texttt{PauliEvolutionGate} (Qiskit
2.x~\cite{qiskit2024}) is exact.
Angles are trained by COBYLA with multiple random restarts (the standard defense against QAOA's
non-convex angle landscape); we then recompute the statevector incrementally, recording $|\psi|^2$ after
each layer.

\paragraph{Language conditioning (the QNLP bridge).}
The LLM emits a signed per-asset conviction $v_i\in[-1,1]$ from natural-language views; we tilt
$\mu\to\mu+s\,v$, echoing Black--Litterman view-blending~\cite{black1992litterman} but routed into the
\emph{quantum} cost Hamiltonian. Changing the language changes $H_C$ and hence the ground state. For
risk, following~\cite{woerner2019risk,grant2021iqae}, we discretize the loss onto $m$ qubits, load
$\sqrt{\mathrm{pmf}}$, flag the tail above VaR with an \texttt{IntegerComparator}, and estimate
$P(\mathrm{loss}\ge\mathrm{VaR})$ by IQAE at $O(1/\varepsilon)$ vs.\ $O(1/\varepsilon^2)$ scaling;
CVaR (expected shortfall~\cite{rockafellar2000cvar}) follows from the loaded distribution.

\section{Results}
All numbers are emitted by \texttt{paper/experiments.py}; the demo is a well-separated 4-asset instance
($B{=}2$). Supporting figures (depth, optimizer trace, risk) are in the appendix.

\begin{figure}[t]\centering

\caption{State evolution: probability over all $2^n$ candidate selections from the uniform superposition
(top) onto the optimal feasible portfolio (gold, \texttt{1100}$=\{$A,B$\}$) across QAOA layers ($p{=}2$).}
\label{fig:evo}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{The state concentrates on the optimum (Fig.~\ref{fig:evo}).}
The output evolves from uniform (P(opt)$=\Puniform$; layers $0.062\!\to\!0.25\!\to\!\Popt$) to a sharp
peak on the optimum at $p{=}2$: P(opt)$=\Popt$ ($\Conc\times$ a uniform guess), approximation ratio
$\AR$, with best-sample recovery of the exact optimum. Depth $p{=}1$ is insufficient (AR $0.50$);
$p{=}2$ already cracks the instance and $p{=}4$ sharpens it (P(opt)$=\Poptd$, AR $\ARd$; Fig.~\ref{fig:depth}).

\begin{figure}[t]\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{figures/fig4_language.png}
\caption{Language steers the optimizer: as LLM conviction on the lower-return pair rises, the optimum
migrates $\{$A,B$\}\!\to\!\{$A,C$\}\!\to\!\{$C,D$\}$ and the QAOA modal selection (\,$\star$\,) tracks it.}
\label{fig:lang}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Language steers the ground state (Fig.~\ref{fig:lang}).}
From the neutral optimum $\{$A,B$\}$, a graded LLM bullish view migrates the exact optimum
$\{$A,B$\}\!\to\!\{$A,C$\}\!\to\!\{$C,D$\}$, and the QAOA modal selection tracks it in all three regimes
(approximation ratios $0.92/0.92/0.85$). Concentration is naturally lower under a strong view
(P(opt)$\,{\approx}\,0.16$--$0.19$) because the tilt creates near-ties among portfolios---an honest
reflection of real ambiguity, not optimizer failure. To our knowledge this is the first demonstration of
a natural-language view visibly deforming the state evolution of a quantum portfolio optimizer.

\paragraph{Risk replication.}
The IQAE circuit loads a fat-tailed (Student-$t$) loss onto 5 qubits at $\alpha{=}0.95$. The
quantum-estimated quantity is the tail-exceedance probability $P(\mathrm{loss}\ge\mathrm{VaR})$, which
IQAE recovers to within $\TailErr\%$ of the loaded distribution (Fig.~\ref{fig:risk}); CVaR then follows
from that distribution, reproducing the Woerner--Egger primitive.

\section{Related Work, Limitations, and Conclusion}
Our core is QAOA~\cite{farhi2014qaoa}; the CVaR-objective variational idea is~\cite{barkoutsos2020cvar};
the risk circuit replicates~\cite{woerner2019risk} with the IQAE estimator of~\cite{grant2021iqae}, in the
lineage of quantum option pricing~\cite{stamatopoulos2020option} and the IBM survey~\cite{egger2020finance}.
QNLP~\cite{coecke2010dispho,lorenz2023qnlp} motivates language as a first-class quantum input; we differ
by routing \emph{LLM-derived} views into a finance optimizer's Hamiltonian and foregrounding
\emph{visualization}. \textbf{Limitations:} results are on small ($n{=}4$--$8$), well-separated,
exactly-simulated instances; we make no scaling or supremacy claim (brute force is our oracle), the
language tilt is a simple additive mechanism, and the IQAE advantage is asymptotic in $\varepsilon$.
\textbf{Conclusion:} making a quantum optimizer's state evolution visible---and showing language steer
it---offers an auditable template for high-stakes quantum finance and a concrete QNLP $\times$
quantum-optimization use case, reproducible from \texttt{python -m paper.experiments}.
